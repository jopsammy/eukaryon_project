July 26, 2026 — Eukaryon Project is officially launched.

To build an agent system, we must first answer three questions: **why is single-step capability insufficient? How do we engineer structural lower bounds? Where are the boundaries of governance?** Three core papers — from theoretical foundation to engineering implementation to governance architecture — are now all in place.

We believe these assessments: the potential of LLMs is far from fully unleashed — the bottleneck is not the foundation model, but the engineering. Long-horizon practice requires not stronger single-step reasoning, but structural guarantees that sustain continuity across checkpoints. A good agent system is first and foremost a system with a complete governance structure.

The goal of Eukaryon Project is simple: **build the most usable agent system in the world.** We mean this.
