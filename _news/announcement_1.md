经过长期准备，Eukaryon Project 官方网站今日正式上线。

After extensive preparation, the official website of Eukaryon Project is now live.

本站承载我们对 Agent 系统的完整思考与工程实践——从长程实践理论到 AC 范式 V6 技术报告，再到 Eureka Agent 三阶段路线规划。

This site carries our complete thinking and engineering practice on agent systems — from long-horizon practice theory, to the AC Paradigm V6 technical report, to the three-phase roadmap for Eureka Agent.

您可以在本站浏览核心论文、了解工程路线、关注项目动态。

Here you can browse our core papers, explore the engineering roadmap, and follow project updates.

Eukaryon Project 的目标：做世界上最好用的 Agent 系统。我们相信 LLM 的潜力在真实长程实践中远未被全面释放——问题不在基座模型，而在工程。

The goal of Eukaryon Project: build the most usable agent system in the world. We believe that the potential of LLMs in real long-horizon practice is far from fully unleashed — the issue is not the foundation model, but the engineering.
