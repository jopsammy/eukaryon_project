Eukaryon Project 的三篇核心论文已在 Zenodo 正式发布，构成从理论到工程的完整闭环。

Three core papers of Eukaryon Project have been published on Zenodo, forming a complete closed loop from theory to engineering.

**理论基座**：《长程实践：超越单次能力的结构下限》，以"当前信息集是否封闭"为严格判据，建立运载前提—6 条逻辑必要条件—7 条工程补偿条件的三层结构下限体系。

**Theoretical foundation**: "Long-Horizon Practice: Structural Lower Bounds Beyond Single-Step Capability" — using "whether the current information set is closed" as a rigorous criterion, establishes a three-tier structural lower bound system.

**工程落地**：《AC 范式 V6 技术报告（TRAE 特调）》，将结构要求转化为可运行的工程中间层——两核文件、GN-004 独立审查、七类外置锚点、Subagent 调度。

**Engineering implementation**: "AC Paradigm V6 Technical Report (TRAE-Tuned)" — translates structural requirements into an operational engineering middle layer: dual-core files, GN-004 independent review, seven categories of external anchors, and subagent scheduling.

**治理架构**：《从黑圣杯到令咒系统》，提出三层同心模型（原核 / 真核 / 皮层），论证离散检查点、权限闸门和机向人请示从"增强"升级为"必需"。

**Governance architecture**: "From the Black Grail to the Command Spell System" — proposes the three-layer concentric model (Prokaryon / Eukaryon / Cortex), arguing that discrete checkpoints, authority gates, and machine-to-human requests upgrade from enhancements to necessities.
