Eukaryon Project 的三篇核心论文已在 Zenodo 平台正式发布，均配有中英文版本：

1. **长程实践：超越单次能力的结构下限** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20470866](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20470866)
2. **从黑圣杯到令咒系统：长程实践的离散本质与Agent治理边界** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21532460](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21532460)
3. **面向长程实践的锚点与契约：AC范式V6技术报告（TRAE特调）** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20471461](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20471461)

Three core papers of the Eukaryon Project have been published on Zenodo, each available in both Chinese and English:

1. **Long-Horizon Practice: Structural Lower Bounds Beyond Single-Step Capability** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20470866](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20470866)
2. **From the Black Grail to the Command Spell System: The Discrete Nature of Long-Horizon Practice and the Boundaries of Agent Governance** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21532460](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21532460)
3. **Anchors and Contracts for Long-Horizon Practice: AC Paradigm V6 Technical Report (TRAE-Tuned)** — DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.20471461](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20471461)

These three papers present our complete thinking on long-horizon practice in Agent systems from three dimensions: theoretical foundation, governance architecture, and engineering implementation. PDFs are available for download on this site.
