### We Are Looking for Companions

We believe that building a truly usable agent system is not something a single company or team can accomplish independently. It requires interdisciplinary collaboration, open discussion, and people willing to dive deep into practice.

If you share these beliefs:

- The potential of LLMs has yet to be fully released—the issue is engineering, not the foundation model
- Agent systems need governance-first architecture design, not just stacking capabilities
- Long-horizon practice requires structural engineering guarantees, not reliance on single-shot model reasoning

Then you are welcome to join us. Whether you are a researcher, engineer, or enthusiast, you can:

- Follow and contribute to [Eukaryon Project](https://github.com/jopsammy/eukaryon_project) on GitHub
- Read our papers and discuss them in academic communities
- Help us improve through real-world usage and feedback

Eukaryon Project is open source and open to all. Together, let's build the most usable agent system.
