论文《长程实践：超越单次能力的结构下限》（Long-Horizon Practice: Structural Lower Bounds Beyond Single-Step Capability）在 Zenodo 发布。

The paper “Long-Horizon Practice: Structural Lower Bounds Beyond Single-Step Capability” has been published on Zenodo.

这是三篇核心论文的理论基座（theoretical foundation）。论文以”当前信息集是否封闭”为严格判据，重新定义长程：不是时间更久，而是关键信息必须在推进过程中逐步生成——由此穷举得到认知缺口、媒介强制、决策分叉与外部漂移四类关键输入来源，建立运载前提—6 条逻辑必要条件—7 条工程补偿条件的三层结构下限体系。

This is the theoretical foundation of our three core papers. Using “whether the current information set is closed” as a rigorous criterion, the paper redefines long-horizon: not about duration, but about critical information that must be gradually generated during the process. From this, it exhaustively identifies four categories of key input sources — epistemic gaps, medium enforcement, decision forks, and external drift — and establishes a three-tier structural lower-bound system: carrier preconditions → 6 logical necessary conditions → 7 engineering compensatory conditions.

核心结论：单次能力强不推出单检查点可完成，单检查点可完成不推出跨检查点持续推进。这不是智力问题，是结构问题。

Core conclusion: strong single-step capability does not imply single-checkpoint completability. Single-checkpoint completability does not imply sustained cross-checkpoint progress. This is not a matter of intelligence — it is a matter of structure.

本文回答三篇论文体系中的第一个问题——为什么单次能力不够？

This paper answers the first question in our three-paper system — why is single-step capability insufficient?
